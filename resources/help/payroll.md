The **Payroll** page lists your pay history, one entry per period, with basic salary, any bonuses or deductions (each with its own reason), the net amount, and a status (e.g. Paid).

Click into an entry to see the full breakdown of adjustments — what each bonus or deduction was for, not just the total.
