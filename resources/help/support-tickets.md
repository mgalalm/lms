title: Opening a support ticket
category: Teaching
audience: teacher
order: 2
---

If a session runs into a technical or attendance problem — a frozen Zoom call, a student who didn't join — click **Support** on that session's row in your Sessions Schedule.

Fill in a subject and a short description of what happened, and a ticket is opened with our support team. From there:

- You'll land on the ticket's own page, which works like a chat thread — replies from support show up there in real time.
- You're notified by email and in-app whenever support replies.
- Once the issue is sorted, click **Resolve** on the ticket to close it out.

Each session can have its own ticket, and past tickets stay linked to that session for reference.
