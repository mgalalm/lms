title: What am I looking at on my dashboard?
category: Getting Started
audience: all
order: 2
---

Your dashboard is the home screen after logging in, and shows different things depending on your role.

**Everyone sees:**
- A **Next Session** card with a countdown and a Join button.
- A **Points/Coins** total and a **Leaderboard** of your peers.

**Students also see:**
- A prompt to rate your last session, once it's finished.
- Your most recent review from a teacher.
- Recently earned **Badges**.

**Teachers also see:**
- A link into the full **Sessions Schedule**.

All times are shown in your own timezone, set on your profile — not the server's or the other person's.
