---
title: How do notifications work?
category: Getting Started
audience: all
order: 3
---

The bell icon in the top-right corner shows a red badge when you have unread notifications.

You'll be notified — by email and in-app — when:
- A new session is scheduled with you.
- A support ticket you're involved in gets a reply (teachers).

Click a notification to jump straight to the relevant page; it's automatically marked as read. Use **Mark all read** in the dropdown to clear the badge without opening each one individually.
