Before you can be assigned to teach a course, you need to complete it yourself — including its certification quiz. This isn't just a formality: it's a hard gate, so you understand the material a student will see before you're in front of one.

Your progress lives under the **LMS** tab:

- Work through each course's modules and lessons like a student would.
- Take the certification quiz once you've finished a course's lessons.
- Passing certifies you for that course, and only certified courses can be assigned to you in scheduling.

You can revisit completed courses any time as a refresher.
