You earn **Coins** for things like completing sessions on time and finishing homework tasks. Your running total shows on your dashboard, along with a leaderboard of how you stack up against other students.

Visit **Achievements** to see:

- Every **badge** you've earned, and what it took to earn it.
- The full leaderboard, not just the top few shown on your dashboard.
- How to earn more coins going forward.
