audience: teacher

The **Sessions Schedule** page lists every session assigned to you, split into **Today**, **Upcoming**, and **Past** tabs.

Each row shows the lesson, the student, and the scheduled time in your own timezone. The **Join** button follows the same 15-minutes-before rule as the dashboard's Next Session card.

If something goes wrong during a session — a frozen video call, a student who never showed up — use the **Support** button on that row to open a ticket. See *Opening a support ticket* for details.
