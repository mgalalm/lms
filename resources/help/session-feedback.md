title: Rating your sessions
category: Rewards
audience: student
order: 2
---

After a session finishes, a quick feedback popup appears on your dashboard asking you to rate it with a star rating from 1 to 5.

Your rating helps flag great sessions and catch problems early — and your most recent review shows on your dashboard afterward. If you dismiss the popup, it reappears next time you visit your dashboard until you submit it.
