title: Submitting leave and HR requests
category: Payroll & HR
audience: teacher
order: 2
---

The **Requests** page is where you submit and track anything that needs HR approval: leave, excused time, general HR requests, support inquiries, and resignation.

- Pick the request type, fill in the form (leave requests show your current monthly/annual leave balance right on the form), and submit.
- Track the status of everything you've submitted in the **History** section below, grouped by type.
- Approved/rejected requests show who reviewed them and any notes left.

Leave balances update automatically once a leave request is approved.
