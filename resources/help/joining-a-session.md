---
title: How do I join a session?
category: Getting Started
audience: all
order: 1
---

Your dashboard shows a **Next Session** card with a countdown to your next class.

- The **Join** (student) or **Join Now** (teacher) button opens Zoom directly — no separate Zoom login is needed.
- The button only becomes clickable from **15 minutes before** the session starts until **60 minutes after** it began. Outside that window it's greyed out on purpose.
- Teachers join as the host; students join as a regular participant, with their real name shown instead of a generic Zoom name.

If the button stays disabled right up to your start time, refresh the page — the countdown updates automatically, but a stuck browser tab occasionally needs a nudge.
