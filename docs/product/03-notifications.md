# Notifications

## Email Notifications
- **Automated system emails** — sent from a support address (e.g. support@ischooltech.com) under a branded name (e.g. "iSchool Team").
- **Teacher reassignment alerts** — email informs the student their teacher has changed for an upcoming session, including new teacher's name and expertise.
- **Dashboard access button** — "Login to your Dashboard" CTA button embedded in emails for quick access.
- **Support/reply channel** — students can reply directly to the notification email for help.
